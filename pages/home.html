What's nova?

Nova is a digital gaming platform dedicated to bringing players and developers together through a vast library of games, exclusive releases, and community-driven experiences. Our mission is to make discovering, purchasing, and enjoying games simple, accessible, and exciting. From indie projects created by passionate developers to major blockbuster releases, Nova offers a diverse collection for every type of player. With competitive prices, seasonal events, personalized recommendations, and a growing gaming community, Nova is building a new destination for players around the world. learn more here
